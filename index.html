Biostatistician Workbench

Companion tools to Think Like a Biostatistician: Plain-English Logic for Safety and Clinical Professionals by Dr Hafez Selim, MD, PhD

Before you begin. These tools help you see the logic in each chapter. They do the arithmetic so you do not have to. They do not make decisions.

  • Educational use only. Not medical advice, not clinical decision support, and not for the care of any patient.
  • Not validated software. Not a medical device and not for regulatory submissions. Check any number you use elsewhere with your statistician.
  • All example numbers are made up for teaching, like the numbers in the book. They describe no real product, trial or patient.
  • Nothing leaves your browser. There is no sign-in, no tracking, and nothing you type is sent anywhere.
  • The views expressed are the author's personal views only and do not represent any current or former employer, client, institution, professional society or regulatory body.
